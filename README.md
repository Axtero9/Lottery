## 📜 Focus on Rules & Transparency
The website places the full event guidelines at its core:
1. Full Name & Active Phone Number required at registration.
2. Tickets are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.
3. Official draw date announced once all tickets are filled.
4. Draw deadline on or before **30th November 2026**.
5. 100% fair and transparent random selection protocol.
6. Direct contact and instant cash/UPI transfer to winners.
7. Organiser's decision is final.

## 📲 How to Connect Your WhatsApp Number

### Method 1: On the Website (Instant)
1. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).
2. Click **"WhatsApp Link"** in the top navigation or footer.
3. Enter your phone number (e.g. `9876543210`) and click **Save Link**.
4. Both **"Purchase Now"** buttons will instantly redirect participants to your WhatsApp chat with a pre-filled ticket request message.
