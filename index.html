Official Guidelines

Important Notes & Conditions

Please read through each of the following rules carefully before purchasing your ticket. All participants are held to these guidelines to ensure a fair and enjoyable event.

01

Full Name & Contact Information

When purchasing a ticket, you must provide your full name and an active phone number / WhatsApp contact. This information is directly attached to your assigned ticket serial number and will be used as the sole identifier when drawing and contacting winners.

Verified directly upon WhatsApp ticket confirmation.
02

Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable

Tickets once purchased are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable. Because ticket proceeds form the fixed prize pool, tickets cannot be cancelled, exchanged, or transferred to another individual after issuance.

Protects the integrity and fixed funding of the prize pool.
03

Draw Date Announcement

The exact date and time of the raffle draw will be officially announced to all registered participants once all ticket slots have been filled. Notifications will be shared directly with each participant.

04

Draw Deadline

The draw will be conducted on or before 30th November 2026. If all tickets are allocated prior to this date, the draw date may be scheduled earlier with advance notice to all ticket holders.

Conducted by: 30 November 2026
05

Fair & Transparent Random Draw

Winners will be selected strictly through a random, transparent draw. Each ticket has an equal probability of winning. The draw will take place either in-person at the friends gathering or via a recorded/livestreamed randomizer so all participants can audit the results.

Order of draw: 3rd Prize (₹500) → 2nd Prize (₹800) → 1st Prize (₹1,000).
06

Winner Notification & Cash Payout

Winners will be contacted immediately following the draw using the phone number provided at the time of purchase. Cash prizes will be transferred via direct UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or cash handover within 24 hours.

07

Organiser’s Final Decision

The organiser's decision regarding the conduct, timing, winner verification, and final outcome of the draw will be final and binding. By purchasing a ticket, participants agree in good faith to honor these terms.

Common Questions & Clarifications

Can I buy more than one ticket?

Yes. You can purchase multiple tickets. Each ticket receives its own distinct entry, proportionately increasing your chances.

How do I get my ticket number?

Once you message the organizer via WhatsApp and confirm payment, your unique ticket serial number will be sent to you directly.

What if I miss the live draw?

You don't need to be present during the draw. If your ticket number is drawn, the organizer will phone and message you directly.

How will I receive the prize money?

Direct instant bank transfer via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or physical cash immediately upon winner verification.
